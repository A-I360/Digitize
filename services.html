Common questions

Things you might want to know.

How long does a project take?
A focused landing page usually lands in three to five weeks. A multi-page site or web app typically runs eight to sixteen weeks depending on scope. You get a written timeline before we start.
Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?
Yes. We work remotely across time zones with asynchronous updates and a scheduled weekly call. Pricing can be quoted in Naira or US dollars.
What do you need from us to start?
A clear sense of the problem, access to any existing assets or systems, and one decision-maker who can approve work. We handle the rest, including copy support if you need it.
Can you redesign something that already exists?
Frequently. We can refresh the visual identity, fix experience gaps, improve performance, or replatform entirely — staging the change so search rankings and existing links are preserved.
What happens after launch?
Every project includes a support window for fixes. After that we offer flexible retainers for optimisation, new features and ongoing maintenance — or a clean hand-over to your own team.
Who owns the code?
You do. On final payment you receive the repository, documentation and deployment instructions. There is no proprietary framework you are locked into.
