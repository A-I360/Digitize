Before you write

Questions we get a lot.

Do I need a finished brief?
No. A paragraph describing the problem is plenty. Discovery is where we turn that into a scope, and it is included in every engagement.
How quickly can you start?
Usually within two to three weeks of signing. If your timing is tight, say so in the message — we hold a little capacity for urgent work.
Can you work with our existing site or team?
Yes. We regularly join in-flight projects, take over legacy codebases, or work alongside an internal team on a specific slice of the product.
