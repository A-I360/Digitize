Credits

Made at SYNQ Technological Services

Design, engineering, art direction and audio produced in-house. Custom physics, rendering and networking layers — no game engine. All visuals drawn procedurally at runtime; no image assets. Soundtrack and effects synthesised live with the Web Audio API. Original characters and world. No third-party or licensed art. Built for the web: keyboard, touch and reduced-motion aware.

What's in it

Built properly.

Three hand-authored levels across dawn cliffs, sky ruins and a crystal vault. Movement built for feel: coyote time, jump buffering, variable jump height, tuned air control and apex float. Moving platforms, jump pads, spike hazards, patrol enemies, drones, checkpoints and collectibles. Local two-player co-op on one device, with a camera that keeps both runners in frame. Online race mode over WebSockets — room codes, live positions, interpolated movement and disconnect handling. Fixed-timestep physics so the game plays identically at 60 Hz and 144 Hz. Respects reduced-motion, pauses when the tab is hidden and releases the audio context on exit.
