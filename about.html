Who we are

A small studio built for serious work.

SYNQ Technological Services is an independent digital product studio in Lagos. We design and build websites, web applications, interactive experiences and games — and we stay with them after launch.

The short version

We started in 2022 with a simple frustration: too much digital work looks finished and behaves unfinished. Beautiful on launch day, slow and fragile six months later.

So we built the studio around the opposite instinct. Every project gets senior attention, a real engineering foundation, and a hand-over your team can actually operate. We keep the team small on purpose so the people you meet are the people who build.

We are comfortable being measured. Our own site scores in the high nineties on Lighthouse, runs with zero runtime dependencies, and ships a playable game we wrote ourselves — because we would rather demonstrate capability than describe it.

Our mission

To make digital products that stay fast, stay maintainable and keep earning their keep — and to hand our clients something they genuinely own.

Values

What we hold to.

◆

Craft over volume

We take on fewer projects so each one gets the attention it needs. We would rather do four things well than twelve adequately.

◈

Honest estimates

Fixed scope, fixed price, stated assumptions. If something changes we say so before it becomes a surprise on an invoice.

▣

Built to be maintained

Readable code, documented decisions, no lock-in. You should never need us to make a small change — though we hope you will want to.

✦

Accessible by default

Keyboard paths, focus states, contrast and reduced-motion support are part of the build, not a remediation project later.

How the work happens

Four stages, no mystery.

01

Discovery

A structured conversation about goals, audience and constraints, ending in a written scope and a fixed price.

02

Design

Visual direction, then interface design in the browser. You review real pages, not flat images.

03

Build

Weekly demos on a live staging URL. Nothing is a surprise at the end because you saw it every week.

04

Launch & grow

Deployment, monitoring, documentation and a support window. Then optional retainers for iteration.
